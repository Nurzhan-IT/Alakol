4. BOOKING PROCEDURE
4.1. The booking process in the eKol system includes 6 stages:

Hotel search by check-in/check-out dates, number of guests
Room selection with viewing available room types and prices
Availability check with automatic verification of available rooms
Data entry with guest contact information
Advance payment N% through Robokassa
Confirmation with sending data to hotel and client

4.2. Booking is considered confirmed after successful payment according to the public offer terms.
4.3. The User receives booking confirmation to the specified email within 24 hours.
4.4. Unpaid bookings automatically expire 10 minutes after creation to complete payment.
5. PAYMENT AND PRICING
5.1. Payment is made through the Robokassa payment system according to the rates specified on the Site.
5.2. Under the flexible eKol payment scheme:

when booking, an advance payment of N% of the total cost is charged, where N can be 10%, 30%, 50%, 70%, or 100% depending on the specific hotel's policy
the remaining (100% - N%) is paid upon check-in at the hotel
when N = 100%, no additional payment at the hotel is required
the eKol platform commission is included in the advance payment

5.3. Prices on the Site may change depending on seasonality, hotel occupancy, and other factors with dynamic pricing application.
5.4. All payments are made in tenge (KZT) or other currencies according to the exchange rate of the National Bank of RK.

5.5. IMPORTANT ROUNDING NOTICE: Due to percentage calculations when calculating advance payments, additional payments, and refunds, all monetary amounts may be rounded with an error of up to 1 (one) tenge in either direction.

6. BOOKING CANCELLATION AND REFUNDS
6.1. Booking cancellation conditions are determined by the specific hotel's policy and are indicated during booking. Booking modification requests are only possible 15 days before check-in.
6.2. In case of booking cancellation by the User's initiative, depending on the advance payment amount, the following refund conditions apply:
With advance payment N = 10%:

The first 10% of advance payment is non-refundable in non-force majeure cases (guarantee deposit)
Cancellation at any time before check-in: 0% refund

With advance payment N = 30%:

The first 10% of advance payment is non-refundable in non-force majeure cases (guarantee deposit)
Refund up to 20% of (30% - 10%) = 20%
Booking cancellation 15 days before check-in: 20% refund
Booking cancellation less than 15 days before check-in: 0% refund
