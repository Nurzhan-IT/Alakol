PAYMENT AND REFUND RULES
ALAKOL HOTEL BOOKING SYSTEM

Effective Date: 19.06.2025
Document Version: 1.0

1. GENERAL PROVISIONS
1.1. These Rules govern the payment procedures for booking services and refund procedures in the Alakol hotel booking system.
1.2. The Rules are an integral part of the User Agreement and are mandatory for all system users.
1.3. By making a payment, the user confirms agreement with these Rules.
1.4. The eKol system supports multilingual functionality (Kazakh, Russian, English languages) and operates in the Kazakhstan/Astana time zone (UTC+5).
2. PAYMENT METHODS
2.1. Available payment methods:

Bank cards (Visa, MasterCard, MIR)
Electronic wallets
Bank transfers
Mobile payments
Other methods supported by the Robokassa payment system

2.2. Payment currencies:

Primary currency: Kazakhstani tenge (KZT)
Supported: Russian ruble (RUB), US dollar (USD), Euro (EUR)
Conversion is performed at the payment system's rate at the time of transaction

2.3. Payment security:

All payments are processed through the certified Robokassa system
Compliance with PCI DSS security standards
Data encryption using SSL protocol
The system does not store bank card data

3. PAYMENT STRUCTURE
3.1. Flexible prepayment system:

The prepayment amount is determined by each hotel's policy and can be:

10% of the total accommodation cost
30% of the total accommodation cost
50% of the total accommodation cost
70% of the total accommodation cost
100% of the total accommodation cost (full prepayment)

3.2. Payment at hotel:

The additional payment amount is (100% - N%), where N is the prepayment percentage
When N = 100%, no additional payment at the hotel is required
Additional payment is made directly at the hotel upon check-in
Payment methods for additional payment are determined by the hotel's policy

3.3. Platform commission:

When booking a room through the eKol platform, a prepayment of 10%, 30%, 50%, 70%, or 100% of the total booking cost is charged, depending on the conditions selected by the hotel
A booking service commission of 10% of the total booking cost is included in the prepayment amount (including online acquiring commission)
The commission covers order processing costs, platform administration, and technical support
The booking service commission (10%) is non-refundable, as the service is considered provided upon booking confirmation

IMPORTANT NOTICE ABOUT ROUNDING: Due to percentage calculations when calculating prepayment, additional payment, and commissions, all monetary amounts may be rounded with an error of up to 1 (one) tenge in either direction.

4. PAYMENT PROCEDURE
4.1. Payment stages:

Hotel selection and booking parameters
Review of payment and cancellation terms
Filling in booking data
Redirect to Robokassa payment system
Payment method selection and payment data entry
Payment confirmation
Receiving booking confirmation

4.2. Payment confirmation:

Successful payment is confirmed instantly
Confirmation is sent to the specified email
The confirmation includes booking number and payment details
In case of unsuccessful payment, funds are not charged

4.3. Payment receipt:

Electronic receipt is sent to email within 24 hours
Receipt contains all necessary details for reporting
Receipt duplicate can be requested from support service

5. REFUNDS
5.1. Grounds for refund:

Booking cancellation initiated by client (partial refund)
Booking cancellation due to force majeure circumstances (full refund)
Hotel's inability to provide services (full refund)
Technical error during payment (full refund)

5.2. Refund conditions upon cancellation:
For 10% prepayment:

Booking service commission (10%) is non-refundable
No additional refund is provided as prepayment consists entirely of commission
Refund for any cancellation: 0%

For 30% prepayment:

Cancellation 15+ days in advance: 20% refund
Cancellation less than 15 days in advance: 0% refund

For 50% prepayment:

Cancellation 15+ days in advance: 40% refund
Cancellation 10-14 days in advance: 20% refund
Cancellation less than 10 days in advance: 0% refund

For 70% prepayment:

Cancellation 15+ days in advance: 60% refund
Cancellation 10-14 days in advance: 40% refund
Cancellation 7-9 days in advance: 10% refund
Cancellation less than 36 hours in advance: 0% refund

For 100% prepayment:

Cancellation 15+ days in advance: 90% refund
Cancellation 10-14 days in advance: 70% refund
Cancellation 7-9 days in advance: 40% refund
Cancellation 36+ hours in advance: 20% refund
Cancellation less than 36 hours in advance: 0% refund

5.3. Refund procedure:

Submitting refund request through system or support service
Request review within 3 business days
Making refund decision and notifying client
Initiating refund through payment system
Refund is processed within 10 business days from cancellation request approval
In case of booking cancellation, the consumer may submit a request for partial or full refund of booking service commission
Decision is made by eKol platform and hotel based on provided documents

5.4. Refund timeframes:

To bank card: 5-10 business days
To electronic wallet: 1-3 business days
By bank transfer: 3-7 business days
Timeframes may increase depending on the issuing bank

6. DISPUTE SITUATIONS
6.1. Unsuccessful payments:

During technical failures, funds are not charged
If funds were charged but booking was not created - automatic refund
Automatic refund timeframe: 1-3 business days

6.2. Double charges:

In case of double charging, one transaction is automatically cancelled
If automatic cancellation did not occur - contact support service
Duplicate payment refund within 5 business days

6.3. Payment disputes:

All disputes are resolved through pre-trial procedure
Complaint review period: 10 business days
Decisions are made based on documents and system data

7. COMMISSIONS AND ADDITIONAL EXPENSES
7.1. Payment system commissions:

Payment processing commissions are included in the cost
Additional commissions from issuing banks are paid by the client
Commissions are not refunded

7.2. Currency conversion:

Conversion is performed at payment system rate
Rate is fixed at the time of transaction
Exchange rate difference during refund is not compensated

8. SECURITY AND DATA PROTECTION
8.1. Payment data protection:

System does not store bank card data
All operations are conducted through secure connection
Compliance with international security standards

8.2. Transaction monitoring:

Automatic monitoring of suspicious transactions
Additional verification when necessary
Blocking fraudulent transactions
