eKol SYSTEM

Time Zone: Kazakhstan/Astana (UTC+5)

1. DEFINITIONS AND TERMS

1.1. Offer - this document containing the proposal of ИП Жұматаев to enter into a contract for hotel booking services through the eKol system.

1.2. Service Provider - ИП Жұматаев, BIN/IIN: 040701551273, engaged in providing hotel booking services through the eKol web platform.

1.3. Customer - an individual who has reached the age of 18, accepted this offer and entered into a contract with the Service Provider.

1.3.1. Hotel accommodation facility owners - hotels, mini-hotels, guest houses and other accommodation facilities (hereinafter - "Hotel", "Partner").

1.3.2. Personal data operator: ИП Жұматаев, BIN: 040701551273

1.4. Website - ekol.kz website, belonging to the Service Provider, operating on a technological platform.

1.5. Services - a complex of services for searching, selecting and booking hotel rooms in the Alakol region, provided by the Service Provider through the eKol system.

1.6. Booking - room reservation in a hotel for a specific period using the eKol system, including 6 stages: hotel search, room selection, availability check, data entry, N% prepayment (where N is determined by hotel policy) and confirmation.

1.7. The eKol system includes the following modules:

  • hotel - hotel, room, and booking management
  • booking - booking functionality
  • userauths - authentication and user management with OTP system
  • user_dashboard - user personal cabinet
  • robokassa - payment system integration
  • search - hotel search
  • addon - additional functions

2. SUBJECT OF CONTRACT

2.1. The Service Provider provides the Customer with services for searching and booking hotel rooms in the Alakol region in accordance with the terms of this offer through the eKol system.

2.2. The Service Provider acts as an intermediary between the Customer and hotels and is not a supplier of hotel services.

2.3. Specific service parameters (accommodation dates, room type, number of guests) are determined by the Customer when making a booking through the eKol system.

3. CONTRACT CONCLUSION PROCEDURE

3.1. This offer is public in accordance with Article 395 of the Civil Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

3.2. The contract is considered concluded from the moment the Service Provider receives acceptance of the offer.

3.3. Acceptance of the offer is the Customer's performance of the following actions:

  • selecting a hotel and booking parameters in the eKol system;
  • filling out the booking form with personal data;
  • confirming agreement with the offer terms;
  • making prepayment of N% of the total accommodation cost, where N can be 10%, 30%, 50%, 70% or 100% depending on the specific hotel's policy.

3.4. The fact of contract conclusion is confirmed by sending the Customer an electronic letter with booking details within 24 hours.

3.5. Unpaid bookings automatically expire 10 minutes after creation.

4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES

4.1. The Service Provider undertakes to:

  • Provide current information about hotels, rooms and prices through the eKol system.
  • Ensure transmission of booking data to the selected hotel using the eKol system.
  • Provide the Customer with booking confirmation through electronic notifications.
  • Ensure confidentiality of the Customer's personal data in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan on personal data.
  • Provide informational support on booking issues in Kazakh, Russian and English languages.
  • Ensure operation of the eKol system with support for Kazakh, Russian and English languages.

4.2. The Service Provider has the right to:

  • Require the Customer to provide reliable information.
  • Refuse to provide services if the Customer violates the contract terms.
  • Change service prices with notification on the Website using dynamic pricing.

4.3. The Customer undertakes to:

  • Provide reliable and complete information when booking through the eKol system.
  • Make timely payment according to contract terms.
  • Comply with hotel accommodation rules.
  • Notify about changes or cancellation of booking within established timeframes.

4.4. The Customer has the right to:

  • Receive information about booking status through personal cabinet in the eKol system.
  • Modify or cancel booking in accordance with the terms of section 7.
  • Demand refund in cases provided by the contract.

6. SERVICE COST AND PAYMENT PROCEDURE

6.1. The cost of booking services is included in the total accommodation cost specified on the Website.

6.2. Payment is made in the following order according to the flexible eKol scheme:

6.2.1. When booking - prepayment of N% of the total accommodation cost through Robokassa payment system, where N can be:

  • 10% - of the total accommodation cost
  • 30% - of the total accommodation cost
  • 50% - of the total accommodation cost
  • 70% - of the total accommodation cost
  • 100% - full prepayment

6.2.2. Upon hotel check-in - additional payment of remaining (100% - N%) directly to the hotel by any available method. When N = 100%, no additional payment is required.

6.3. Prepayment serves as a guarantee of serious intentions and booking confirmation. The eKol platform commission is included in the prepayment.

6.4. Prepayment payment methods through Robokassa: Visa, MasterCard bank cards and other methods available through Robokassa.

6.6. IMPORTANT NOTICE ABOUT ROUNDING: Due to percentage calculations when calculating prepayment, additional payment and refunds, all monetary amounts may be rounded with an error of up to 1 (one) tenge in either direction.

7. BOOKING MODIFICATION AND CANCELLATION

7.1. The Customer can modify or cancel booking by sending notification to email support@ekol.kz.

7.2. Booking cancellation conditions depending on prepayment amount:

For prepayment N = 10%:

  • First 10% of prepayment is non-refundable in non-force majeure cases (security deposit)
  • Cancellation at any time before check-in: 0% refund

For prepayment N = 30%:

  • First 10% of prepayment is non-refundable in non-force majeure cases (security deposit)
  • Refund up to 20% of (30% - 10%) = 20%
  • Booking cancellation 15 days before check-in: 20% refund
  • Booking cancellation less than 15 days: 0% refund

In case of force majeure circumstances:

In case of force majeure circumstances (natural disasters, military actions, epidemics), prepayment refund is made in full.

7.4. Refund is processed within 10 business days to the bank card from which payment was made.
