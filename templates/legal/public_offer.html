1. Definitions and Terms

1.1. Offer - this document containing the proposal of [LEGAL ENTITY/INDIVIDUAL ENTREPRENEUR NAME] to conclude an agreement for hotel booking services through the HMS (Hotel Management System) Alakol system.

1.2. Contractor - [LEGAL ENTITY/INDIVIDUAL ENTREPRENEUR NAME], BIN/IIN: [BIN/IIN], legal address: [ADDRESS], conducting business activities for providing hotel booking services through the HMS Alakol web platform.

1.3. Customer - an individual who has reached 18 years of age, who has accepted this offer and concluded an agreement with the Contractor.

1.4. Website - the website [WEBSITE ADDRESS], owned by the Contractor, operating on Django 4.2.2 technology platform with PostgreSQL database.

1.5. Services - a set of services for searching, selecting and booking hotel rooms in the Alakol region, provided by the Contractor through the HMS system.

1.6. Booking - room reservation at a hotel for a specific period using the HMS system, including 6 stages: hotel search, room selection, availability check, data entry, 10% prepayment and confirmation.

1.7. The HMS system includes the following modules:

  • hotel - hotel, room, and booking management
  • booking - booking functionality
  • userauths - authentication and user management with OTP system
  • user_dashboard - user personal cabinet
  • robokassa - payment system integration
  • search - hotel search
  • addon - additional functions

2. Subject of Agreement

2.1. The Contractor provides the Customer with services for searching and booking hotel rooms in the Alakol region in accordance with the terms of this offer through the HMS system.

2.2. The Contractor acts as an intermediary between the Customer and hotels and is not a provider of hotel services.

2.3. Specific service parameters (accommodation dates, room type, number of guests) are determined by the Customer when placing a booking through the HMS system.

3. Agreement Formation Process

3.1. This offer is public in accordance with Article 395 of the Civil Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

3.2. The agreement is considered concluded from the moment the Contractor receives acceptance of the offer.

3.3. Acceptance of the offer is the Customer performing the following actions:

  • selecting a hotel and booking parameters in the HMS system
  • filling out the booking form with personal data
  • confirming consent to the offer terms
  • making prepayment of 10% of the total accommodation cost in accordance with Section 6 of this offer

3.4. The fact of agreement conclusion is confirmed by sending the Customer an electronic letter with booking details within 24 hours.

3.5. Unpaid bookings automatically expire 10 minutes after creation.

4. Rights and Obligations

4.1. The Contractor undertakes to:

4.1.1. Provide current information about hotels, rooms and prices through the HMS system.

4.1.2. Ensure transmission of booking data to the selected hotel using the HMS system.

4.1.3. Provide the Customer with booking confirmation through electronic notifications.

4.1.4. Ensure confidentiality of the Customer's personal data in accordance with Kazakhstan legislation on personal data.

4.1.5. Provide information support on booking issues in Kazakh, Russian and English languages.

4.1.6. Ensure HMS system operation with support for Kazakh, Russian and English languages.

4.2. The Contractor has the right to:

4.2.1. Require the Customer to provide accurate information.

4.2.2. Refuse to provide services if the Customer violates the agreement terms.

4.2.3. Change service prices with notification on the Website using dynamic pricing.

4.3. The Customer undertakes to:

4.3.1. Provide accurate and complete information when booking through the HMS system.

4.3.2. Make timely payments according to the agreement terms.

4.3.3. Comply with hotel accommodation rules.

4.3.4. Notify about booking changes or cancellations within established timeframes.

4.4. The Customer has the right to:

4.4.1. Receive booking status information through the personal cabinet in the HMS system.

4.4.2. Change or cancel bookings in accordance with Section 7 terms.

4.4.3. Demand refunds in cases provided by the agreement.

5. Service Description

5.1. Within the framework of service provision, the Contractor:

5.1.1. Provides access to the partner hotel database in the Alakol region through the HMS system.

5.1.2. Processes booking requests using the HMS booking module.

5.1.3. Communicates with hotels for booking confirmation.

5.1.4. Provides information support before, during and after accommodation.

5.1.5. Ensures hotel search and filtering by various criteria through the search module.

5.1.6. Provides booking management system through the user_dashboard module.

5.2. Services do not include:

5.2.1. Hotel accommodation (provided directly by the hotel).

5.2.2. Additional hotel services (meals, spa, excursions, etc.).

5.2.3. Transportation services.

5.2.4. Travel insurance.

6. Service Cost and Payment Procedure

6.1. The cost of booking services is included in the total accommodation cost specified on the Website.

6.2. Payment is made in the following order according to the HMS scheme:

6.2.1. When booking - prepayment of 10% of the total accommodation cost through the Robokassa payment system.

6.2.2. Upon check-in at the hotel - payment of the remaining 90% directly to the hotel by any available method.

6.3. Prepayment serves as a guarantee of serious intentions and booking confirmation.

6.4. Prepayment methods through Robokassa:

  • Visa, MasterCard, МИР bank cards
  • electronic wallets
  • bank transfers
  • other methods available through Robokassa

6.5. When paying by bank card, funds are debited at the moment of payment confirmation using PCI DSS protection technologies.

6.6. Payment receipts are provided electronically to the Customer's email.

6.7. Prices may change depending on seasonality, hotel occupancy and other factors using the dynamic pricing system.

7. Booking Changes and Cancellation

7.1. The Customer may change or cancel a booking by sending notification to email [EMAIL] or through the personal cabinet in the HMS system.

7.2. Booking cancellation conditions:

7.2.1. Booking Cancellation:

  • prepayment of 10% of the total cost is non-refundable
  • considered as payment for booking services

7.2.2. No-show:

  • prepayment is non-refundable
  • considered a contract violation

7.3. Force majeure circumstances:

In case of force majeure circumstances (natural disasters, military actions, epidemics, government decisions restricting movement), prepayment refund is made in full.

7.4. Changing dates or room type is possible subject to availability and may incur additional payment for the cost difference.

7.5. Refunds are processed:

  • to bank card within 5-10 business days
  • to electronic wallet within 1-3 business days

8.1. The Contractor is liable for:

8.1.1. Timely transmission of booking data to the hotel through the HMS system.

8.1.2. Providing accurate information about accommodation conditions.

8.1.3. Safekeeping of the Customer's personal data in a protected PostgreSQL database.

8.2. The Contractor is not liable for:

8.2.1. Quality of services provided by the hotel.

8.2.2. Actions/inactions of hotel staff.

8.2.3. Changes in hotel operations independent of the Contractor.

8.2.4. Technical failures of payment systems.

8.2.5. Force majeure circumstances.

8.3. The Contractor's maximum liability is limited to the amount of prepayment paid by the Customer.

8.4. The Customer is liable for:

8.4.1. Accuracy of provided information.

8.4.2. Timely payment of services.

8.4.3. Compliance with hotel accommodation rules.

9. Force Majeure

9.1. Parties are released from liability for non-performance of obligations if this is caused by circumstances of irresistible force.

9.2. Force majeure circumstances include:

  • natural disasters
  • military actions
  • terrorist acts
  • government decisions
  • epidemics and quarantine restrictions

9.3. In case of force majeure circumstances, prepayment refund is made in full.

10. Personal Data Protection

10.1. Personal data processing is carried out in accordance with the Privacy Policy and the Law of Kazakhstan "On Personal Data and Their Protection".

10.2. By concluding the agreement, the Customer consents to the processing of the following categories of personal data:

  • account data (full name, email, phone, gender, country, city, address, photo)
  • booking data (guest full name, contact details, code country, preferences)
  • technical data (browser and device information, cookies, visit logs)
  • behavioral data (hotel search history, room selection preferences, interaction analytics)

10.3. Personal data is transferred to hotels only to the extent necessary for ensuring accommodation, and to the Robokassa payment system for payment processing.

11. Dispute Resolution

11.1. Parties take all measures to resolve disputes through negotiations.

11.2. If pre-trial settlement is impossible, disputes are considered in the courts of Kazakhstan at the Contractor's location.

11.3. Kazakhstan legislation applies to the parties' relations.

12. Agreement Term

12.1. The agreement comes into force from the moment of its conclusion and is valid until the parties fully fulfill their obligations.

12.2. Individual agreement provisions (warranty obligations, liability) may remain in effect after completion of main obligations.

13. Final Provisions

13.1. The Contractor has the right to unilaterally change the offer terms with notification on the Website 3 days in advance.

13.2. Changes do not apply to already concluded agreements.

13.3. If any provision of the offer is found invalid, other provisions remain in force.

13.4. The agreement is available in Kazakh, Russian and English. In case of discrepancies, the version in the state language of Kazakhstan takes precedence.
