BOOKING AND CANCELLATION RULES

eKol HOTEL BOOKING SYSTEM

Effective Date: June 19, 2025
Document Version: 1.0

1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1. These Rules define the terms and procedures for booking hotel rooms, as well as the conditions for modifying and canceling bookings through the website [WEBSITE ADDRESS].

1.1.1. In these Rules, "owners of accommodation facilities" means hotels, mini-hotels, guest houses, and other accommodation facilities (hereinafter - "Hotel", "Partner").

1.1.2. Personal Data Operator: IP [IP Name], BIN: [BIN]

1.2. The Rules apply to all users of the booking system and are an integral part of the Public Offer for hotel booking services.

1.3. By accepting the booking terms, the User agrees to these Rules in full.

2. BOOKING CONDITIONS

2.1. Minimum requirements for booking:

  • Registration on the website or providing contact information
  • Selecting hotel, room type, and accommodation dates
  • Agreement with hotel terms and these Rules
  • Payment of advance payment in the amount established by the hotel

2.2. Advance payment amount:

  • The advance payment amount is determined by each hotel independently
  • Advance payment can be 10%, 30%, 50%, 70%, or 100% of the total cost
  • The remaining amount is paid at the hotel upon check-in
  • With 100% advance payment, no additional payment at the hotel is required

2.3. Booking confirmation:

  • Booking is considered confirmed after successful payment of the advance payment
  • Confirmation is sent to the specified email within 15 minutes
  • The confirmation includes all booking details and hotel contacts

3. CANCELLATION CONDITIONS

IMPORTANT: Cancellation conditions depend on the amount of advance payment made and the time until check-in.

3.1. General cancellation principles:

  • The first 10% of advance payment serves as a guarantee deposit and is non-refundable upon customer-initiated cancellation
  • The remaining portion of advance payment may be refunded depending on cancellation timing
  • In case of force majeure circumstances, the advance payment is refunded in full

3.2. Cancellation conditions by advance payment amount:

With 10% advance payment:

  • Cancellation at any time: 0% refund (entire amount remains as guarantee deposit)
  • No-show: 0% refund

With 30% advance payment:

  • Cancellation 15+ days before check-in: 20% refund
  • Cancellation 10-14 days before check-in: 0% refund
  • Cancellation less than 10 days before check-in: 0% refund
  • No-show: 0% refund

With 50% advance payment:

  • Cancellation 15+ days before check-in: 40% refund
  • Cancellation 10-14 days before check-in: 20% refund
  • Cancellation 7-9 days before check-in: 0% refund
  • Cancellation less than 7 days before check-in: 0% refund
  • No-show: 0% refund

With 70% advance payment:

  • Cancellation 15+ days before check-in: 60% refund
  • Cancellation 10-14 days before check-in: 40% refund
  • Cancellation 7-9 days before check-in: 10% refund
  • Cancellation less than 36 hours before check-in: 0% refund
  • No-show: 0% refund

With 100% advance payment:

  • Cancellation 15+ days before check-in: 90% refund
  • Cancellation 10-14 days before check-in: 70% refund
  • Cancellation 7-9 days before check-in: 40% refund
  • Cancellation 36+ hours before check-in: 20% refund
  • Cancellation less than 36 hours before check-in: 0% refund
  • No-show: 0% refund

3.3. Force majeure circumstances:

  • Natural disasters
  • Military actions or terrorist acts
  • Epidemics and pandemics with movement restrictions
  • Hotel closure by government authorities
  • Other emergency situations recognized as force majeure

In case of force majeure circumstances, the advance payment is refunded in full regardless of cancellation timing.

4. CANCELLATION PROCEDURE

4.1. Submitting cancellation request:

  • Request is submitted through personal account on the website
  • Or by email with booking number
  • Or by phone to customer support

4.2. Request processing:

  • Requests are processed within 1-2 business days
  • Customer receives cancellation confirmation by email
  • The confirmation specifies the refund amount

4.3. Refund process:

  • Refund is made using the same payment method used for the original payment
  • Refund period: 5-10 business days
  • Payment system fees are not refunded

5. BOOKING MODIFICATION

5.1. Possible modifications:

  • Changing accommodation dates (subject to room availability)
  • Changing room type
  • Changing number of guests

5.2. Modification conditions:

  • Modifications are accepted no later than 15 days before check-in
  • If the cost increases, the difference must be paid
  • If the cost decreases, the difference is not refunded
  • An administrative fee may be charged for booking modifications

6. SPECIAL CONDITIONS

6.1. Group bookings:

  • Bookings for 5 or more rooms are considered group bookings
  • Special cancellation conditions may apply to group bookings
  • Conditions are agreed individually with the hotel

6.2. Holiday periods:

  • Special conditions may apply during holiday and peak periods
  • Hotels may set stricter cancellation conditions
  • Special conditions are indicated during booking

7. RESPONSIBILITIES OF PARTIES

7.1. Booking system responsibilities:

  • Ensuring the operation of the booking platform
  • Correct transmission of information to hotels
  • Processing payments and refunds
  • Technical support for users

7.2. Hotel responsibilities:

  • Providing booked rooms
  • Compliance with stated accommodation conditions
  • Quality of services provided
  • Compliance with cancellation and refund conditions

7.3. Customer responsibilities:

  • Providing correct information when booking
  • Timely payment of advance payment
  • Compliance with hotel rules
  • Notification of cancellation within established timeframes

7.4. Responsibility for overbooking:

  • The hotel bears full responsibility for overbooking as it failed to provide timely updates on room availability
  • In case of overbooking, the Hotel must independently resolve the customer accommodation issue at its own expense (providing alternative accommodation of equal or higher class, compensation)
  • The eKol platform does not bear responsibility for overbooking and serves only as an intermediary in providing information

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1. Dispute resolution procedure:

  • All disputes are resolved through pre-trial procedures
  • Claims processing period: 10 business days
  • Decisions are made based on documents and system data

8.2. Complaint submission:

  • Through customer support service
  • By email with detailed description
  • Through personal account on the website

9. FINAL PROVISIONS

9.1. These Rules may be amended by the Company without prior notice. The new version of the Rules takes effect upon posting on the website.

9.2. All matters not regulated by these Rules are resolved in accordance with the current legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

9.3. If any provision of these Rules is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions continue to be in force.
