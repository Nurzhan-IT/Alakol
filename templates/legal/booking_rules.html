1. General Provisions

1.1. These Rules define the terms and procedures for booking hotel rooms, as well as conditions for modifying and canceling bookings through the website [WEBSITE ADDRESS].

1.2. The Rules apply to all users of the booking system and are an integral part of the Public Offer for hotel booking services.

1.3. By accepting booking conditions, the User agrees to these Rules in full.

2. Booking Conditions

2.1. Minimum Booking Requirements:

2.1.1. User age - at least 18 years old.

2.1.2. Providing accurate personal data:

  • full name
  • valid phone number
  • email address
  • identification documents if required

2.1.3. Agreement to Terms of Use and Privacy Policy conditions.

2.2. Booking Procedure:

2.2.1. Select hotel and room type for desired dates.

2.2.2. Check room availability for selected dates.

2.2.3. Fill out booking form specifying:

  • check-in and check-out dates
  • number of adults and children
  • contact information
  • special requests (if any)

2.2.4. Confirm data and review conditions.

2.2.5. Make prepayment of 10% of the total cost.

2.3. Booking Confirmation Timeframes:

2.3.1. Automatic confirmation - within 5 minutes for partner hotels.

2.3.2. Manual confirmation - within 24 hours for other hotels.

2.3.3. If no rooms available, booking is declined with full prepayment refund.

2.4. Check-in Documents:

2.4.1. For Kazakhstan citizens - identity card or passport.

2.4.2. For foreign citizens - passport with visa (if required).

2.4.3. For children - birth certificate or passport.

3. Payments and Pricing

3.1. Payment Structure:

3.1.1. Prepayment when booking - 10% of total cost.

3.1.2. Balance payment upon check-in - 90% of total cost.

3.1.3. Cost includes:

  • room reservation
  • platform commission
  • applicable taxes

3.2. Dynamic Pricing:

3.2.1. Prices may change depending on:

  • seasonality
  • hotel occupancy
  • demand for specific dates
  • special events in the city

3.2.2. Price is fixed at the moment of booking confirmation.

3.3. Additional Fees:

3.3.1. Tourist tax (if applicable) - paid upon check-in.

3.3.2. Room deposit (if required by hotel) - paid upon check-in.

3.3.3. Additional hotel services are paid separately.

4. Cancellation Rules

4.1. Cancellation Classification:

4.1.1. Timely cancellation - 24 hours or more before check-in date.

4.1.2. Late cancellation - less than 24 hours before check-in date.

4.1.3. No-Show - failure to arrive at hotel without prior notification.

4.2. Refund Conditions:

4.2.1. Timely cancellation (more than 24 hours):

  • 100% prepayment refund
  • no penalty charges
  • refund timeframe: 5-10 business days

4.2.2. Late cancellation (less than 24 hours):

  • 50% prepayment refund
  • 50% penalty charge
  • refund timeframe: 5-10 business days

4.2.3. No-Show:

  • no refund provided
  • prepayment fully retained
  • room may be given to another guest after 06:00 next day

4.3. Special Cancellation Conditions:

4.3.1. Holiday bookings:

  • cancellation possible no later than 72 hours before
  • later cancellation results in no refund

4.3.2. Group bookings (more than 5 rooms):

  • individual cancellation conditions
  • hotel coordination required

4.3.3. Special offers and promotions:

  • may have cancellation restrictions
  • conditions specified when booking

5. Booking Modifications

5.1. Possible Changes:

5.1.1. Change of accommodation dates - subject to room availability.

5.1.2. Change of room type - with additional payment/refund of cost difference.

5.1.3. Change in number of guests - within room capacity limits.

5.1.4. Shortening stay period - according to partial booking cancellation rules.

5.2. Modification Conditions:

5.2.1. Change request must be submitted no later than 24 hours before check-in.

5.2.2. Changes possible only with available rooms.

5.2.3. Additional payment for changes (if cost increases) made before check-in.

5.2.4. Refund of difference (if cost decreases) - within 10 business days.

5.3. Modification Restrictions:

5.3.1. Cannot change hotel after booking confirmation.

5.3.2. Date changes may be impossible during high season.

5.3.3. Some special rates are non-modifiable.

6. Special Situations

6.1. Force Majeure Circumstances:

6.1.1. Natural disasters, military actions, epidemics.

6.1.2. Government decisions restricting movement.

6.1.3. In force majeure cases, full prepayment refund is provided.

6.2. Hotel Issues:

6.2.1. Hotel closure due to circumstances beyond our control.

6.2.2. Lack of rooms upon check-in due to hotel fault.

6.2.3. In such cases, alternative accommodation is offered or full refund provided.

6.3. Technical Failures:

6.3.1. Payment system failures.

6.3.2. Booking confirmation problems.

6.3.3. Technical support works on quick problem resolution.

7. Cancellation and Refund Procedure

7.1. Methods for Submitting Cancellation Request:

7.1.1. Through personal cabinet on website.

7.1.2. By email [EMAIL].

7.1.3. By phone [PHONE] during business hours.

7.2. Required Information in Request:

  • booking confirmation number
  • guest full name
  • contact details
  • cancellation reason
  • preferred refund method

7.3. Processing Timeframes:

7.3.1. Request confirmation - within 2 hours.

7.3.2. Request processing - 1-3 business days.

7.3.3. Refund processing - 5-10 business days to original payment method.

7.4. Refund Methods:

7.4.1. Bank card - 5-10 business days.

7.4.2. Electronic wallet - 1-3 business days.

7.4.3. Bank transfer - 3-7 business days.

8. Guest Responsibilities

8.1. Provide accurate and complete information when booking.

8.2. Arrive at hotel on confirmed check-in date.

8.3. Have valid identification documents for check-in.

8.4. Comply with hotel rules and policies.

8.5. Pay remaining balance upon check-in (90% of total cost).

8.6. Notify about any changes or cancellations in advance.

8.7. Respect other guests and hotel property.
