1.5. The eKol system consists of the following main modules:

  • hotel - hotel, room, and booking management
  • booking - booking functionality with 6 process stages

  • user_dashboard - user personal account
  • robokassa - payment system integration
  • search - hotel search and filtering
  • addon - additional functions

2. SERVICE DESCRIPTION

2.1. The Company provides Users with information services for searching and booking hotel rooms in the Alakol region, Republic of Kazakhstan through the eKol system.

2.2. Booking is carried out based on a public offer posted on the Site.

2.3. The Company acts as an intermediary between the User and hotels and is not a provider of hotel services.

2.4. The eKol system provides the following main functions:

  • User registration and authentication with OTP system for verification
  • Hotel search and filtering by various criteria
  • Viewing detailed information about hotels and rooms
  • Room booking system with availability checking
  • Integrated payment system through Robokassa
  • Booking management through personal account
  • Email or SMS notification system
  • Multilingual interface (Kazakh, Russian, English languages)

3. REGISTRATION AND ACCOUNT

3.1. To get full access to the Site functions, it is necessary to register in the eKol system.

3.2. When registering, the User is obliged to provide the following mandatory data:

  • Email (used as username, unique)
  • Password
  • Username

3.3. Additional profile data includes:

  • Full name (full_name)
  • Phone number (phone)
  • Gender (gender) - male/female
  • Country of residence (country)
  • City of residence (city)
  • Address (address)
  • Profile photo (image)

4. BOOKING PROCEDURE

4.1. The booking process in the eKol system includes 6 stages:

  1. Hotel search by check-in/check-out dates, number of guests
  2. Room selection with viewing available room types and prices
  3. Availability check with automatic verification of available rooms
  4. Data entry with guest contact information
  5. Advance payment N% through Robokassa
  6. Confirmation with sending data to hotel and client

4.2. Booking is considered confirmed after successful payment according to the public offer terms.

4.3. The User receives booking confirmation to the specified email within 24 hours.

4.4. Unpaid bookings automatically expire 10 minutes after creation to complete payment.

5. PAYMENT AND PRICING

5.1. Payment is made through the Robokassa payment system according to the rates specified on the Site.

5.2. Under the flexible eKol payment scheme:

  • when booking, an advance payment of N% of the total cost is charged, where N can be 10%, 30%, 50%, 70%, or 100% depending on the specific hotel's policy
  • the remaining (100% - N%) is paid upon check-in at the hotel
  • when N = 100%, no additional payment at the hotel is required
  • the eKol platform commission is included in the advance payment

5.3. Prices on the Site may change depending on seasonality, hotel occupancy, and other factors with dynamic pricing application.

5.4. All payments are made in tenge (KZT) or other currencies according to the exchange rate of the National Bank of RK.

5.5. IMPORTANT ROUNDING NOTICE: Due to percentage calculations when calculating advance payments, additional payments, and refunds, all monetary amounts may be rounded with an error of up to 1 (one) tenge in either direction.

6. BOOKING CANCELLATION AND REFUNDS

6.1. Booking cancellation conditions are determined by the specific hotel's policy and are indicated during booking. Booking modification requests are only possible 15 days before check-in.

6.2. In case of booking cancellation by the User's initiative, depending on the advance payment amount, the following refund conditions apply:

With advance payment N = 10%:

  • The first 10% of advance payment is non-refundable in non-force majeure cases (guarantee deposit)
  • Cancellation at any time before check-in: 0% refund

With advance payment N = 30%:

  • The first 10% of advance payment is non-refundable in non-force majeure cases (guarantee deposit)
  • Refund up to 20% of (30% - 10%) = 20%
  • Booking cancellation 15 days before check-in: 20% refund
  • Booking cancellation less than 15 days before check-in: 0% refund

7.3. Responsibility for overbooking:

  • In case of overbooking, the Hotel must independently resolve the customer accommodation issue at its own expense (providing alternative accommodation of equal or higher class, compensation)
  • The eKol platform does not bear responsibility for overbooking and serves only as an intermediary in providing information
