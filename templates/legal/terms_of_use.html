4. Booking Process

4.1. The booking process in the eKol system includes 6 stages:

  1. Hotel Search: Search by city, check-in/check-out dates, number of guests
  2. Room Selection: Choose room with viewing available room types and prices
  3. Availability Check: Automatic verification of available rooms
  4. Data Entry: Fill in guest contact information
  5. Payment: Pay 10% prepayment through Robokassa
  6. Confirmation: Send data to hotel and client

4.2. Booking is considered confirmed after successful payment according to the public offer terms.

4.3. The User receives booking confirmation to the specified email within 24 hours.

4.4. Unpaid bookings automatically expire 10 minutes after creation to complete payment.
