PRIVACY POLICY

ALAKOL HOTEL BOOKING SYSTEM

Effective Date: June 19, 2025
Document Version: 1.0

System: eKol - Hotel Management System
Payment System: Robokassa (official integration)
Region: Alakol, Republic of Kazakhstan
Time Zone: Kazakhstan/Astana (UTC+5)

1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1. This Privacy Policy (hereinafter - "Policy") defines the procedure for processing personal data of users of the eKol system, located at ekol.kz (hereinafter - "Website").

1.2. The personal data operator is ИП Жұматаев, BIN: 040701551273 (hereinafter - "Operator", "Company", "we").

1.3. Personal data processing is carried out in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Personal Data and their Protection" and other regulatory legal acts of RK.

1.4. By using the eKol system, you consent to the processing of your personal data in accordance with this Policy.

2. LEGAL GROUNDS FOR PERSONAL DATA PROCESSING

2.1. The legal grounds for personal data processing are:

  • consent of the personal data subject;
  • necessity of contract performance to which the personal data subject is a party;
  • necessity of compliance with the Operator's legal obligations;
  • protection of vital interests of the personal data subject.

3. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL DATA

3.1. We process the following categories of personal data according to the eKol system User model:

3.1.1. Account data:

  • email address (email) - used as username, unique;
  • gender (gender) - male/female;
  • country and city of residence (country, city);
  • residential address (address);
  • password (in encrypted form);
  • profile photo (image);
  • OTP code for verification;
  • electronic wallet (wallet) - for future bonus programs;
  • verification status (verified).

3.1.2. Booking data according to the Booking model:

  • guest's email (email);
  • country code (country_code);
  • selected hotel (hotel);
  • room type (room_type);
  • specific rooms (room) - many-to-many relationship;
  • selection data (selection_data) - JSON field;
  • check-in and check-out dates (check_in_date, check_out_date);
  • number of days (total_days);
  • number of adults and children (num_adults, num_children);
  • check-in/check-out statuses (checked_in, checked_out);
  • unique booking ID (booking_id);
  • Robokassa transaction ID (robokassa_inv_id);
  • preferences and special requests.

3.1.3. Payment information:

  • bank card data (processed by Robokassa payment system);
  • payment and transaction information;
  • payment identifiers;
  • amount before discount (before_discount);
  • total amount (total);
  • savings amount (saved);
  • payment status (payment_status);
  • IMPORTANT ROUNDING NOTICE: Due to percentage calculations when calculating advance payments, additional payments, and refunds, all monetary amounts may be rounded with an error of up to 1 (one) tenge in either direction;
  • applied coupons (coupons).

3.1.4. Technical data:

  • browser and device information;
  • cookie files;
  • user visit and action logs;
  • session times;
  • system fields: booking activity (is_active), creation time (created_at), expiration time (expires_at).

3.1.5. Behavioral data:

  • hotel search history;
  • room selection preferences;
  • service usage frequency;
  • eKol system interface interaction analytics.

4. PURPOSES OF PERSONAL DATA PROCESSING

4.1. Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

4.1.1. Providing booking services:

  • processing booking requests through the eKol system booking module;
  • confirming and modifying bookings (modification possible only 15 days before check-in);
  • communication with hotels for accommodation organization.

4.1.2. Ensuring eKol system operation:

  • user registration and authentication through the userauths module;
  • OTP system for verification;
  • interface personalization;
  • security provision;
  • management through the user_dashboard module.

4.1.3. Communications:

  • sending booking confirmations;
  • informing about order changes;
  • technical support in Kazakh, Russian, and English languages;
  • marketing messages (with consent).

4.1.4. Analytics and service improvement:

  • analyzing eKol system usage through search and addon modules;
  • functionality improvement;
  • statistical research.

4.1.5. Compliance with legal obligations:

  • fulfilling RK legislation requirements;
  • fraud prevention;
  • dispute resolution.

5. METHODS OF PERSONAL DATA PROCESSING

5.1. Personal data processing is carried out using automation tools and without their use.

5.2. Processing methods include: collection, systematization, accumulation, database storage; clarification, modification, use; distribution, transfer, anonymization, blocking, deletion.

6. PERSONAL DATA PROCESSING TERMS

6.1. Personal data is processed for the following terms:

  • 6.1.1. Account data - until submission of consent withdrawal request or account deletion.
  • 6.1.2. Booking data - for 5 years after completion of the last booking for accounting reporting and dispute resolution purposes.
  • 6.1.3. Technical data - for 1 year from the last visit to the eKol system.
  • 6.1.4. Payment information - in accordance with RK financial legislation requirements and Robokassa policy.
  • 6.1.5. Unpaid bookings - automatically deleted 10 minutes after payment deadline expiration.

7. TRANSFER OF PERSONAL DATA TO THIRD PARTIES

7.1. We may transfer personal data to the following third parties:

  • 7.1.1. Partner hotels in the Alakol region to ensure booking and accommodation.
  • 7.1.2. Robokassa payment system for payment processing using secure SHA256 digital signatures.
  • 7.1.3. IT service providers to ensure eKol system operation on the platform.
  • 7.1.4. AWS email provider for notification sending.
  • 7.1.5. Hosting providers to ensure website operation.
  • 7.1.6. Government agencies in cases provided by RK legislation.

8. CROSS-BORDER TRANSFER OF PERSONAL DATA

8.1. Cross-border transfer of personal data may be carried out to countries providing adequate level of personal data protection.

8.2. When transferring data to other countries, we use appropriate protection guarantees in accordance with RK legislation requirements.

9. SECURITY MEASURES

9.1. We apply technical and organizational measures to protect personal data:

9.1.1. Technical measures:

  • data encryption during transmission (SSL/TLS);
  • password hashing;
  • regular backup;
  • protection against unauthorized access;
  • security monitoring;
  • CSRF attack protection;
  • user input validation and sanitization.

9.1.2. Organizational measures:

  • access restriction to personal data;
  • staff training on personal data protection issues;
  • internal security policies;
  • security system audits;
  • limited access to personal data;
  • administrator action logging.

10. PERSONAL DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

10.1. Personal data subjects have the right to:

  • obtain information about personal data processing;
  • request access to their personal data;
  • request correction of inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of personal data;
  • request restriction of processing;
  • object to processing;
  • data portability;
  • withdraw consent to processing;
  • file complaints with supervisory authorities.

11. EXERCISING RIGHTS

11.1. To exercise your rights, you can contact us:

  • by email: support@ekol.kz;
  • through the feedback form on the website;
  • by mail to the legal address.

11.2. We will respond to your request within 30 days from the date of receipt.

12. DATA RETENTION

12.1. We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to achieve the processing purposes.

12.2. After the retention period expires, personal data is securely deleted or anonymized.

13. PRIVACY POLICY CHANGES

13.1. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

13.2. The updated Policy is posted on our website and takes effect immediately upon posting.

13.3. We recommend that you regularly review this Policy to stay informed about our data processing practices.
