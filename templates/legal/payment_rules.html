1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1. Purpose

These Rules define the procedure for payment of hotel booking services and conditions for refund of money when using the HMS (Hotel Management System) for the Alakol region via the website [WEBSITE ADDRESS].

1.2. HMS Alakol Platform

HMS Alakol is a modern web platform for hotel booking, built on Django 4.2.2 (Python web framework) technology with PostgreSQL database and integrated Robokassa payment system.

1.3. Legal Framework

The Rules are developed in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan and are an integral part of the Public Offer for booking services.

1.4. Agreement Acceptance

By using the service and making payment, the User agrees to these Rules in full.

1.5. System Features

The HMS system supports multilingual functionality (Kazakh, Russian, English languages) and operates in the Kazakhstan/Astana time zone (UTC+5).

1.6. Architecture

The system architecture includes seven specialized modules: hotel (hotel management), booking (reservation), userauths (authentication), user_dashboard (personal cabinet), robokassa (payments), search (search), addon (additional functions).

3. PAYMENT STRUCTURE

3.1. Innovative "10% + 90%" Payment Scheme

HMS Alakol uses a unique payment structure:

3.2. Prepayment at Booking Confirmation

  • Amount: Exactly 10% of total accommodation cost
  • Purpose: Guarantee of serious intentions and booking confirmation
  • Processing: Through integrated Robokassa payment system
  • Currency: Tenge (KZT) or other available currencies
  • Payment deadline: 10 minutes from booking creation (automatic expiration if not paid)

3.3. Balance Payment at Hotel Check-in

  • Amount: Remaining 90% of total accommodation cost
  • Payment method: Directly at the hotel by any convenient method
  • Currency: According to specific hotel policy
  • Payment time: At check-in

3.4. Additional Fees and Taxes

  • VAT: Included in stated price (when applicable)
  • Platform commission: Included in total cost
  • Payment system fees: No fees charged to User for bank card payments
  • E-wallet fees: According to respective system tariffs
  • Bank transfer fees: According to bank tariffs

3.5. Resort Fee (Tourist Tax)

  • Not included in booking cost
  • Paid separately at hotel check-in
  • Amount determined by local legislation

4. HMS PAYMENT PROCESS

4.1. Six-Stage HMS Alakol Booking Process

  1. Search hotels by city, check-in/check-out dates, number of guests
  2. Select specific room and view detailed information
  3. Automatic availability check for selected rooms
  4. Fill guest contact details with OTP verification
  5. Confirm terms and proceed to 10% prepayment

4.2. Technical Payment Processing Features

  • Automatic hotel system notification about confirmed booking
  • Instant confirmation sent to client via email and SMS

6. REFUND CONDITIONS

IMPORTANT NOTICE: In accordance with HMS Alakol policy, the 10% prepayment is NON-REFUNDABLE for voluntary booking cancellation by the User, except for force majeure circumstances.

6.1. Refund Grounds and Compensation Amounts

6.1.1. Voluntary Booking Cancellation by User

  • Refund: 0% (no refund)
  • Justification: prepayment serves as guarantee of serious intentions
  • Applicability: regardless of cancellation time

6.1.2. Force Majeure Circumstances

  • Refund: 100% of prepayment amount
  • Circumstances: natural disasters, emergencies, border closures by government decision
  • Documentation: required
  • Processing time: priority (1-3 business days)

6.1.3. Hotel Closure Due to Independent Reasons

  • Refund: 100% of prepayment amount
  • Reasons: sanitary measures, repair works, license revocation
  • Alternative: equivalent accommodation offer

6.1.4. HMS System Technical Failures

  • Refund: 100% of erroneously charged amounts
  • Reasons: double charging, system errors, Robokassa integration failures
  • Refund time: immediate (within 1-3 business days)

6.1.5. Service Non-Compliance with Stated Characteristics

  • Refund: 100% of prepayment amount with documentary confirmation
  • Grounds: significant room non-compliance with description, absence of stated amenities
  • Procedure: justified complaint filing with evidence
