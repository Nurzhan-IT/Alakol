1. General Provisions

1.1. This Agreement governs the relationship between the Hotel Booking Platform "Alakol Booking System" (hereinafter - "Platform") and owners of hotel accommodation facilities (hereinafter - "Hotel", "Partner").

1.2. Platform Details:

1.3. The Platform acts as an intermediary between Hotels and customers, providing technological infrastructure for room booking.

1.4. This Agreement is a public offer in accordance with Article 395 of the Civil Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

2. Subject of Agreement

2.1. The Platform provides the Hotel with access to the eKol (Hotel Management System) booking management system, including:

  • Web interface for posting hotel information
  • Online room booking system
  • Integration with Robokassa payment system
  • Multilingual support (Kazakh, Russian, English languages)
  • Notification and reporting system

2.2. The Hotel grants the Platform the right to place information about its facilities and accept bookings from customers through the Platform.

3. Payment Scheme and Commission

3.1. Current Payment Scheme:

  • Customer makes a prepayment of 10% of the total accommodation cost through the Platform
  • The remaining 90% is paid by the customer directly at the Hotel upon check-in

3.2. Platform Commission:

  • Platform withholds commission from each successful booking
  • Commission rate is agreed individually (recommended rate: 8-12%)
  • Commission is charged on the full booking cost, including additional payment at hotel

3.3. Settlements between Parties:

Calculation Example:
Prepayment from customers → Platform Account via Robokassa
Settlement with Hotel → Monthly by the 15th of the following month
Payment amount: (Prepayment + Additional payment at hotel) - Platform Commission

4. Hotel Obligations

4.1. Providing Current Information:

  • Maintaining current hotel data (name, address, contacts)
  • Updating room, price and availability information
  • Providing high-quality photos of rooms and premises
  • Specifying exact check-in and check-out times

4.2. Booking Terms Compliance:

  • Guarantee availability of booked rooms for specified dates
  • Ensure check-in for customers with confirmed bookings
  • Accept 90% additional payment upon check-in
  • Provide fiscal documents for payment

4.3. Customer Service:

  • Maintain service quality standards
  • Respond to customer requests within 24 hours
  • Inform Platform of booking problems
  • Maintain accurate check-in reporting

4.4. Legal Compliance:

  • Hold all necessary licenses and permits
  • Comply with fire safety and sanitary standards
  • Maintain accounting and reporting in accordance with RK tax legislation
  • Ensure security of customer personal data

5. Platform Obligations

5.1. Technical Support:

  • Maintain booking system operability 24/7
  • Ensure payment transaction security
  • Provide technical support to Hotel
  • Regular updates and functionality development

5.2. Marketing and Promotion:

  • Place Hotel information on platform
  • Search results optimization
  • Conduct marketing campaigns
  • Provide analytics and reports

5.3. Booking Processing:

  • Transfer booking information to Hotel
  • Process payments through Robokassa
  • Send notifications to customers and Hotel
  • Maintain booking history

6. Data Management and Confidentiality

6.1. Customer Personal Data:

  • Platform transfers to Hotel only necessary data for check-in
  • Hotel undertakes to use data exclusively for service provision
  • Both parties comply with RK Law "On Personal Data and Their Protection"

6.2. Commercial Information:

  • Parties undertake not to disclose confidential information
  • Data on prices, room availability and customers is confidential
  • Confidentiality obligation remains valid for 3 years after Agreement termination

7. Liability of Parties

7.1. Hotel Liability:

  • For quality of accommodation services provided
  • For compliance of actual conditions with those stated on Platform
  • For timely provision of rooms to customers
  • For damage caused by non-performance of obligations

7.2. Platform Liability:

  • For correct transfer of booking information
  • For security of payment processing
  • For preservation of personal data in system
  • For technical service availability (99.5% uptime guarantee)

7.3. Liability Limitation:

  • Platform is not liable for Hotel service quality
  • Hotel is not liable for Platform technical failures
  • Maximum damage amount limited to commission amount for last 3 months

8. Booking Cancellation Terms

8.1. Standard Cancellation Terms:

Cancellation Condition Prepayment Refund
Cancellation more than 24 hours before check-in Prepayment is not refunded
Cancellation less than 24 hours before check-in Prepayment is not refunded
No-show Prepayment is not refunded

8.2. Force Majeure Circumstances:

  • Hotel closure due to reasons beyond Hotel's control
  • Government restrictions on movement
  • Natural or man-made emergency situations
  • In specified cases prepayment is fully refunded to customer

8.3. Refund Procedure:

  1. Refund application submitted through Platform
  2. Review period: up to 10 business days
  3. Refund processed through the same method as original payment

9. Taxation and Reporting

9.1. Hotel Tax Obligations:

  • Hotel independently maintains booking income accounting
  • Declares all income in accordance with RK Tax Code
  • Provides customers with fiscal documents for additional payments
  • Upon request provides Platform with tax payment certificates

9.2. Platform Tax Obligations:

  • Platform maintains commission income accounting
  • Declares income from intermediary activities
  • Provides Hotel with commission payment certificates
  • Cooperates with tax authorities during inspections

9.3. Ensuring Transparency:

  • Detailed accounting of all transactions through system is maintained
  • Additional checks by tax authorities are possible
  • Both parties undertake to provide full reporting upon request

10. Term and Termination

10.1. Term of Validity:

  • Agreement comes into force from the moment of Hotel registration on Platform
  • Valid until termination by one of the parties
  • Automatically renewed in absence of termination notice

10.2. Grounds for Termination:

  • Mutual consent of parties
  • Unilateral termination with 30 days notice
  • Material breach of Agreement terms
  • Liquidation or bankruptcy of one of the parties

10.3. Termination Procedure:

  1. Sending written notice
  2. Completion of all active bookings
  3. Final settlement between parties
  4. Removal of Hotel information from Platform (upon request)

11. Dispute Resolution

11.1. Pre-trial Procedure:

  • All disputes resolved through negotiations
  • Claim review period: 15 business days
  • Mandatory sending of written claim

11.2. Judicial Procedure:

  • When pre-trial settlement is impossible, disputes are transferred to court
  • Applicable law: legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan
  • Jurisdiction: courts of Almaty, Republic of Kazakhstan
