COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH "ALAKOL BOOKING SYSTEM" HOTEL BOOKING PLATFORM

Place: Almaty, Republic of Kazakhstan

1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1. This Agreement governs the relationship between the hotel booking platform "Alakol Booking System" (hereinafter - "Platform") and owners of hotel accommodation facilities (hereinafter - "Hotel", "Partner").

1.3. The Platform acts as an intermediary between Hotels and clients, providing technological infrastructure for room booking.

1.4. This Agreement is a public offer in accordance with Article 395 of the Civil Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

2. SUBJECT OF THE AGREEMENT

2.1. The Platform provides the Hotel with access to the eKol booking management system (Hotel Management System), including:

  • Web interface for posting hotel information
  • Online room booking system
  • Integration with Robokassa payment system
  • Multilingual support (Kazakh, Russian, English languages)
  • Notification and reporting system

2.2. The Hotel grants the Platform the right to post information about its facilities and accept bookings from clients through the Platform.

3. PAYMENT SCHEME AND COMMISSION REMUNERATION

3.1. Flexible payment scheme:

  • The client makes an advance payment of N% of the total accommodation cost through the Platform, where N is determined by the specific hotel's policy and may be:
    • 10% of the total accommodation cost
    • 30% of the total accommodation cost
    • 50% of the total accommodation cost
    • 70% of the total accommodation cost
    • 100% of the total accommodation cost (full advance payment)
  • The remaining (100% - N%) is paid by the client directly to the Hotel upon check-in
  • When N = 100%, no additional payment at the hotel is required

3.2. Platform commission remuneration:

  • When booking a room through the eKol platform, an advance payment is charged at 10%, 30%, 50%, 70%, or 100% of the total booking cost, depending on the conditions selected by the hotel
  • Booking service commission of 10% of the total booking cost is included in the advance payment cost (including online acquiring commission)
  • The commission covers order processing costs, platform administration, and technical support
  • The booking service commission (10%) is non-refundable as the service is considered rendered upon booking confirmation
  • The remaining part of the advance payment is refunded according to booking cancellation conditions

3.2.1. IMPORTANT ROUNDING NOTICE: Due to percentage calculations when calculating advance payments, additional payments, commissions, and transfers to the hotel, all monetary amounts may be rounded with an error of up to 1 (one) tenge upward or downward.

3.3. Settlements between parties:

  • Advance payments from clients are received to the Platform's account through Robokassa
  • Client payment: Advance payment N% (including platform commission) + Additional payment at hotel (100% - N%)
  • The hotel receives N% minus platform commission immediately after client check-in

4. HOTEL OBLIGATIONS

4.1. Providing current information:

  • Maintaining current hotel data (name, address, contacts)
  • Updating information about rooms, prices, and availability
  • Providing quality photos of rooms and premises
  • Specifying exact check-in and check-out times

4.2. Compliance with booking conditions:

  • Guarantee availability of booked rooms for specified dates
  • Ensure accommodation of clients with confirmed bookings
  • Accept additional payment of (100% - N%) upon check-in, where N is the advance payment amount
  • When N = 100%, no additional payment is required
  • Provide fiscal documents for payment

4.3. Client service:

  • Maintain service quality standards
  • Respond to client requests within 24 hours
  • Inform the Platform about booking problems
  • Maintain correct reporting on check-ins

4.4. Legal compliance:

  • Have all necessary licenses and permits
  • Comply with fire safety and sanitary standards requirements
  • Maintain accounting and reporting in accordance with RK tax legislation
  • Ensure security of client personal data

5. PLATFORM OBLIGATIONS

5.1. Technical support:

  • Maintaining booking system functionality
  • Ensuring payment transaction security
  • Providing technical support to the Hotel
  • Regular updates and functionality development

5.2. Marketing and promotion:

  • Posting Hotel information on the platform
  • Search result optimization
  • Conducting marketing campaigns

5.3. Booking processing:

  • Transmitting booking information to the Hotel
  • Processing payments through Robokassa
  • Maintaining booking history

6. DATA MANAGEMENT AND CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1. Client personal data:

  • The Platform transfers to the Hotel only necessary data for accommodation
  • The Hotel undertakes to use data exclusively for service provision
  • Both parties comply with requirements of the RK Law "On Personal Data and Their Protection"

6.2. Commercial information:

  • Parties undertake not to disclose confidential information
  • Data on prices, room availability, and clients are confidential
  • Confidentiality obligation is valid for 3 years after Agreement termination

7. PARTY LIABILITY

7.1. Hotel liability:

  • For quality of provided accommodation services
  • For compliance of actual conditions with those stated on the Platform
  • For timely provision of rooms to clients
  • For damage caused by non-fulfillment of obligations
  • For overbooking - full responsibility is borne by the owner of the hotel accommodation facility, as the hotel did not provide timely current data on room availability
  • In case of overbooking, the Hotel must independently resolve the client accommodation issue at its own expense (providing alternative accommodation of equal or higher class, compensation)

7.2. Platform liability:

  • For correct transmission of booking information
  • For payment processing security
  • For personal data preservation in the system
  • For technical service availability

7.3. Liability limitation:

  • The Platform is not liable for quality of Hotel services
  • The Hotel is not liable for Platform technical failures
  • Maximum damage amount is limited to commission amounts for the last 3 months

8. BOOKING CANCELLATION CONDITIONS

8.1. Standard cancellation conditions depending on advance payment amount:

With advance payment N = 10%:

  • Booking service commission (10%) is not refunded
  • Additional refund is not provided as the advance payment fully consists of commission
  • Cancellation at any time before check-in: 0% refund
  • No-show: 0% refund

With advance payment N = 30%:

  • First 10% of advance payment is not refunded in NON-force majeure circumstances (guarantee deposit)
  • Refund up to 20% of (30% - 10%) = 20%
  • Conditions by cancellation time before check-in:
    • Booking cancellation 15 days before check-in: 20% refund
    • Booking cancellation 10 days before check-in: 0% refund
    • Booking cancellation 7 days before check-in: 0% refund
    • Booking cancellation 36 hours before check-in: 0% refund
    • Booking cancellation less than 36 hours before check-in: 0% refund
  • No-show: same conditions apply

With advance payment N = 50%:

  • First 10% of advance payment is not refunded in NON-force majeure circumstances (guarantee deposit)
  • Refund up to 40% of (50% - 10%) = 40%
  • Conditions by cancellation time before check-in:
    • Booking cancellation 15 days before check-in: 40% refund
    • Booking cancellation 10 days before check-in: 20% refund
    • Booking cancellation 7 days before check-in: 0% refund
    • Booking cancellation 36 hours before check-in: 0% refund
    • Booking cancellation less than 36 hours before check-in: 0% refund
  • No-show: same conditions apply

With advance payment N = 70%:

  • First 10% of advance payment is not refunded in NON-force majeure circumstances (guarantee deposit)
  • Refund up to 60% of (70% - 10%) = 60%
  • Conditions by cancellation time before check-in:
    • Booking cancellation 15 days before check-in: 60% refund
    • Booking cancellation 10 days before check-in: 40% refund
    • Booking cancellation 7 days before check-in: 10% refund
    • Booking cancellation 36 hours before check-in: 0% refund
    • Booking cancellation less than 36 hours before check-in: 0% refund
  • No-show: same conditions apply

With advance payment N = 100%:

  • First 10% of advance payment is not refunded in NON-force majeure circumstances (guarantee deposit)
  • Refund up to 90% of (100% - 10%) = 90%
  • Conditions by cancellation time before check-in:
    • Booking cancellation 15 days before check-in: 90% refund
    • Booking cancellation 10 days before check-in: 70% refund
    • Booking cancellation 7 days before check-in: 40% refund
    • Booking cancellation 36 hours before check-in: 20% refund
    • Booking cancellation less than 36 hours before check-in: 0% refund
  • No-show: same conditions apply

8.2. Force majeure circumstances:

  • Hotel closure due to reasons beyond the Hotel's control
  • Introduction of government restrictions on movement
  • Emergency situations of natural or man-made nature (natural disasters, military actions, epidemics)
  • In specified cases, advance payment is fully refunded to the client:
    • With N = 10%: 10% refund
    • With N = 30%: 30% refund
    • With N = 50%: 50% refund
    • With N = 70%: 70% refund
    • With N = 100%: 100% refund

8.3. Refund procedure:

  • Refund is made within 10 business days from the moment of cancellation request approval
  • In case of booking cancellation, the consumer may submit a request for partial or full refund of booking service commission
  • Decision is made by eKol platform and hotel based on provided documents
  • Refund is made using the same method as the original payment

9. TAXATION AND REPORTING

9.1. Hotel tax obligations:

  • Hotel independently maintains accounting of booking revenues
  • Declares all revenues in accordance with the Tax Code of RK
  • Provides fiscal documents to clients for additional payments
  • Upon request, provides Platform with tax payment certificates

9.2. Platform tax obligations:

  • Platform maintains accounting of commission revenues
  • Declares revenues from intermediary activities
  • Provides Hotel with certificates of paid commissions
  • Cooperates with tax authorities during inspections

10. TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1. Term:

  • Agreement comes into force from the moment of Hotel registration on the Platform
  • Valid until termination by one of the parties
  • Automatically renewed in absence of termination notice

10.2. Grounds for termination:

  • Mutual consent of parties
  • Unilateral termination with 30 days' notice
  • Material breach of Agreement conditions
  • Liquidation or bankruptcy of one of the parties

10.3. Termination procedure:

  • Sending written notice
  • Completing all active bookings
  • Final settlement between parties
  • Removing Hotel information from the Platform

11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

11.1. Pre-trial procedure:

  • All disputes are resolved through negotiations
  • Claim consideration period: 15 business days
  • Mandatory sending of written claim

11.2. Judicial procedure:

  • When pre-trial settlement is impossible, disputes are referred to court
  • Applicable law: legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan

11.3. Dispute resolution for room unavailability:

  • In case a room cannot be provided for selected booking dates, the Hotel must:
    • First contact the eKol Platform and report this incident immediately after problem identification
    • Then contact the client who booked the room at their hotel
    • The Hotel undertakes to resolve this incident through negotiations with the client (offering alternative accommodation, date transfer, compensation, etc.)
    • Negotiation results with the client must be reported to the Platform with necessary evidence (correspondence, documents, confirmations)
    • Provided information is necessary for correct booking data changes in the system
  • Booking change requests are accepted only 15 days before check-in

12. FINAL PROVISIONS

12.1. Changes and additions:

  • The Platform has the right to change Agreement conditions.
  • Notification of material changes (e.g., commission or settlement condition changes) is sent 15 calendar days in advance, for non-material changes — 7 calendar days in advance via email specified during registration or through personal account.
  • Continued work with the platform after changes come into effect means consent to new conditions.
  • Hotel owner has the right to terminate the Agreement in case of disagreement with changes, notifying the Platform within 7 days from receiving the notification.

12.2. Other conditions:

  • Agreement is made in Russian language
  • Translations to Kazakh and English languages are for reference purposes
  • Electronic signature has legal force
  • All appendices are integral parts of the Agreement

Contact information:

  • Email for Hotels: support@ekol.kz
  • Technical support: support@ekol.kz

Agreement comes into force from the moment of accepting its conditions during Platform registration.
