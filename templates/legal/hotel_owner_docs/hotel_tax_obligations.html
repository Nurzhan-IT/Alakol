1. General Tax Requirements

1.1. Applicable Legislation:

  • Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (Code "On taxes and other obligatory payments to the budget")
  • Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On tourist activities in the Republic of Kazakhstan"
  • Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On state regulation of tourism development in the Republic of Kazakhstan"
  • Government Resolutions of the Republic of Kazakhstan on taxation issues

1.2. Tax Status of Hotel Owners:

  • All hotel owners must be registered as taxpayers
  • Conducting business without registration is a violation of tax legislation
  • Mandatory filing of tax returns within established deadlines

1.3. Types of Applicable Tax Regimes:

  • General taxation (income tax 20%)
  • Simplified declaration (subject to compliance with conditions)
  • VAT (when exceeding threshold values)
  • Social tax on payroll

2. Taxation of Booking Revenue

2.1. Hotel Revenue Structure:

  • 10% prepayment goes to Platform account (commission agent)
  • 90% additional payment goes directly to the hotel
  • Platform commission is deducted from the total amount

2.2. Hotel Taxable Base:

  • Full cost of accommodation services (100%) is subject to taxation
  • Platform commission reduces the taxable base as an expense
  • Prepayment through Platform is included in hotel income

2.3. Tax Calculation Example:

Accommodation cost: 100,000 tenge
Prepayment (10%): 10,000 tenge (through Platform)
Additional payment (90%): 90,000 tenge (at hotel)
Platform commission (10%): 10,000 tenge

Hotel income: 100,000 tenge
Hotel expenses: 10,000 tenge (commission)
Taxable base: 90,000 tenge
Income tax (20%): 18,000 tenge

3. Documentation and Accounting

3.1. Mandatory Documents for Accounting:

  • Accommodation service contracts
  • Fiscal receipts when receiving additional payments
  • Statements from Platform about transferred funds
  • Reconciliation acts with Platform

3.2. Platform Revenue Accounting:

  • Maintaining booking register with amount specifications
  • Reconciling Platform data with internal accounting
  • Monthly reconciliation of settlements with Platform
  • Documentation of all financial transactions

4. Income Declaration

4.1. Declaration Filing Deadlines:

  • Monthly declarations: by the 15th of the following month
  • Annual declaration: by March 31st of the following year
  • Quarterly social tax reports: by the 15th

4.2. Declaration Content:

  • All income from accommodation services
  • Expenses related to service provision
  • Commissions paid to Platform
  • Social contributions on employee salaries

5. Platform Interaction on Tax Matters

5.1. Platform Obligations:

  • Providing statements about transferred funds
  • Maintaining detailed records of all transactions
  • Providing documents to tax authorities upon request
  • Notification of changes in tax accounting

5.2. Hotel Obligations:

  • Correct reflection of all income in accounting
  • Timely filing of declarations
  • Providing documents to tax authorities
  • Notifying Platform of tax inspections

6. Tax Control and Inspections

6.1. Preparation for Tax Inspections:

  • Maintaining complete documentation
  • Regular data reconciliation with Platform
  • Timely elimination of identified violations
  • Consultations with tax consultants

7. VAT and Threshold Values

7.1. VAT Application Conditions:

  • When exceeding turnover of 30,000 MCI per year (for 2025)
  • Mandatory VAT payer registration
  • VAT rate: 12% (standard rate)

8. Social Contributions and Labor Relations

8.1. Social Tax on Payroll:

  • Rate: 9.5% on payroll
  • Mandatory pension contributions: 10%
  • Mandatory social contributions: 3.5%
  • Mandatory health insurance contributions

9. Penalties and Liability

9.1. Administrative Penalties:

  • Late declaration filing: 10 MCI
  • Incomplete income reflection: 50% of additional assessment
  • Conducting business without registration: 100 MCI
  • Non-compliance with tax payment deadlines: penalty 1.25% per month

10. Tax Planning

10.1. Tax Payment Optimization:

  • Choosing optimal tax regime
  • Planning expenses to reduce taxable base
  • Using tax benefits (when available)
  • Correct application of depreciation deductions

10.2. Business Seasonality Accounting:

  • Planning income and expenses by months
  • Creating reserves for tax payments during high season
  • Optimizing tax payments considering seasonality
